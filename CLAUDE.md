# Meirav Boaz marketing: instructions for Claude

Read this file at the start of every session. It is the shared memory between sessions.

## What this repo is
Knowledge, principles and logs for marketing Meirav Boaz's music business
(adult music courses and private lessons in Ness Ziona, plus children's shows).

## Files
- `business.md`: the business, offers, audience, contact details, accounts and IDs
- `principles.md`: what works and what doesn't. Update it when a campaign teaches something.
- `campaigns/log.md`: one entry per campaign (dates, goal, budget, audience, creative, results, lessons)
- `website/changelog.md`: every change made to meiravboaz.co.il

## Working rules
- **Never launch, activate, or raise the budget of a campaign without explicit approval.** Drafts and paused campaigns are fine.
- **Never send email or messages on Merav's behalf without approval.**
- Website changes: edit the `meravboaz/meiravboaz-site` repo. A push to `main` goes live on meiravboaz.co.il within about a minute through Netlify.
  Describe the change and get approval before pushing anything visitors will see.
- After any change, add a line to the right log in this repo and push it.
- Ad copy and site copy are in Hebrew (RTL). Keep the warm, encouraging, "it's never too late" tone of the site.
- Keep logs factual: numbers, dates, what was decided and why.
