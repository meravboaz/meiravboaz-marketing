# Principles

What works and what doesn't. Each line should come from real results or a decision Merav made.
Format: `- [YYYY-MM-DD] principle (evidence / source)`

## Ads
- [2026-11-03] Google disapproves phone numbers written in ad text or sitelink descriptions; use the call asset only (sitelink "צרו קשר" disapproval)
- [2026-10-24] Meta "Traffic" objective with automatic placements spent 63% on Instagram Reels and brought ~1,350 clicks but no measurable leads; use a Leads objective with feed placements (Sept–Oct 2026 Meta campaign)

## Audience and targeting
- [2026-10-24] Meta Advantage+ audience pushed 22% of spend to ages 25–34 although the course is for older adults; keep it off and set ages 55+ (Sept–Oct 2026 Meta campaign)

## Creative and copy
- [2026-11-02] Merav's rule: "שיעור ניסיון ללא עלות" may appear on the site and in ad body text, but never as an ad headline, highlighted callout/sitelink, form title or button

## Website and conversion
- Meta feed images: make them 4:5 (1080x1350) or 1:1. Facebook crops taller images in the feed, and in Merav's poster that cut the phone line (2026-11-07).
- Google Search: generic senior-leisure keywords (גיל הזהב, פעילויות לגמלאים) spent 71% of the budget on people looking for trips, lectures and day centers, with no leads (first week, 2026-11). Keep keywords music-specific.
